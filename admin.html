Manage the Website

Demo mode. Changes you make here are saved in this browser so you can show how the site works. On the live site, changes are published for everyone through a secure account.

Post News

Fill in a title and a short message. A photo and a button are optional. When you press Publish, the item appears at the top of the News page.

Choose a photo from your computer or phone. If you skip this, a decorative image is used.

Upload This Week's Bulletin

Choose the bulletin PDF (the same file you print), give it a title and date, and press Publish. It appears at the top of the Bulletin page.

PDF files up to about 4 MB work best for the demo.
